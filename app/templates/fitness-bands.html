Gadgetbridge

  • What it is: Free, open-source Android app that replaces vendor companion apps for supported wearables.
  • Data storage: All health data (steps, heart rate, sleep, SpO2) stored locally in a SQLite database on your phone.
  • Export: Built-in CSV/DB export. Integrates with OpenTracks and FitoTrack for workout logging.
  • Install: Available on F-Droid (recommended) or build from source.
  • Supported devices: PineTime, Xiaomi/Mi Band series, Amazfit Bip/Band/GTR/GTS series, Casio, Fossil, and many more.

Bridging to Home Assistant

  • Tasker + MQTT: Use Gadgetbridge broadcast intents → Tasker → MQTT publish → Home Assistant sensor entities.
  • Direct DB read: Periodically sync the Gadgetbridge SQLite file to your server and parse with a custom sensor.
  • REST API: Export data from Gadgetbridge and POST to Home Assistant's REST API or a local InfluxDB for Grafana dashboards.

🏆 #1: Movesense HR2 (8.05)

The technical user's dream. ECG-grade R-R intervals (gold standard HRV), zero cloud dependency, fully open firmware and API. The catch: no built-in recovery/sleep algorithms — you build your own pipeline. Chest-strap form factor. Perfect for developers and researchers who want absolute data sovereignty.

🥇 #2: Oura Ring 4 (7.37)

Best consumer experience. r²=0.98 HRV accuracy vs ECG, excellent sleep/recovery, screen-free ring, GDPR+HIPAA, free CSV export. Trade-off: $6/mo for API access.

🥈 #3: Amazfit Helio Strap (7.31)

Best value screen-free HRV strap. Dedicated BioTracker 6.0, full recovery suite, no subscription, $99. Held back by Amazfit's poor privacy and "tinkerer-only" Gadgetbridge.

Best hybrid watch: Withings ScanWatch 2 (7.14) — medical-grade ECG in a classy analog design, public REST API, French/EU privacy, polysomnography-calibrated sleep. Near screen-free aesthetics.

Best ring (no US restriction): Ultrahuman Ring Air (7.14) — tied with Withings. No subscription, Gadgetbridge protocol documented, UltraSignal raw data. Not available in US currently.

Best premium watch: Garmin Fenix 8 (6.77) — deepest recovery/training ecosystem, FIT files via USB without cloud, partial Gadgetbridge. Expensive and has a screen, but unmatched data accessibility.

Best fully-local band: Xiaomi Smart Band 9 (6.57) — 21-day battery, full Gadgetbridge, cheapest path to local-only data.

WHOOP 5.0 (6.26) — best recovery science in the industry, but subscription lock-in and data hostage model keep it mid-pack.

Recommendations by Use Case

  • HRV purist + technical: Movesense HR2 — ECG-grade R-R intervals, zero cloud, fully open. Build your own analysis with raw data. The gold standard if you can handle DIY.
  • Best consumer all-rounder: Oura Ring 4 — best HRV accuracy (r²=0.98), polished sleep/recovery, screen-free ring, free CSV export, HIPAA/GDPR compliant.
  • Best value screen-free strap: Amazfit Helio Strap — $99, dedicated HRV, full recovery suite, no subscription. Partial Gadgetbridge support trending toward full local.
  • Best hybrid watch: Withings ScanWatch 2 — looks like a regular watch, medical-grade ECG, public REST API, French/EU privacy. Best for professionals who want discreet health tracking.
  • Best premium watch: Garmin Fenix 8 — deepest training/recovery ecosystem, FIT files via USB (no cloud needed), partial Gadgetbridge. For serious athletes with budget.
  • Best fully-local band: Xiaomi Smart Band 9 — $35, 21-day battery, full Gadgetbridge, strongest local-only story among consumer bands.
  • Best no-sub ring outside US: Ultrahuman Ring Air — Gadgetbridge protocol documented, UltraSignal raw data, no subscription. Unavailable in US due to patent dispute.
  • Most open / hackable: Bangle.js 2 — JavaScript-programmable, 543+ community apps, official Gadgetbridge, 4-week battery. Perfect for building your own health stack.
