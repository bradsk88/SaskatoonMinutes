About this site

YXEMinutes is an independent, volunteer-run project that reads the public record of Saskatoon City Council and committee meetings and turns it into plain-language summaries, so residents can follow civic decisions without watching hours of video.

Not affiliated with the City

This site is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the City of Saskatoon or eSCRIBE Software Ltd. All data comes from the City's public eSCRIBE portal.

The official record wins

Summaries here are generated automatically and may contain errors, omissions, or mischaracterizations of what was said or decided. They are a reading aid, not a record. For anything that matters — legal, financial, property, or personal decisions — rely on the official agenda, minutes, and video on the City's portal, which every summary links back to.

Errors and removals

If a summary misrepresents what you said at a meeting, or you would like your name removed from a page, email bradjohnson.se@gmail.com and it will be corrected or taken down promptly.
