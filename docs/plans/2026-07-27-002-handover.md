## The evidence Brad decided on

Generated on three real fixture items with the live key. Bullets are
better when an item has several distinct facts:

> *Vacant Lot and Adaptive Reuse Incentive Program*
> - New 83-unit apartment building gets five-year tax breaks
> - City will apply for provincial education tax abatement
> - Former transit building becomes theatre, studios, restaurant, retail

and worse when it has one, which is most items:

> *Proposed Rezoning – 902 through 938 3rd Avenue North*
> - City rezones 3rd Avenue North properties
> - It shifts from industrial to residential use
> - This allows for higher-density housing
> - The change aligns with the city's growth plan

The prose it replaced was *"Saskatoon is considering rezoning **902-938**
3rd Avenue North from light industrial to corridor residential, allowing
higher density housing in a growth area."* The bullets **lost the
addresses**, and bullets 2-4 open with "It" / "This" / "The change" —
one sentence chopped into four, not four facts.

So two rules are not optional:

- One bullet per **distinct** fact. One bullet is a valid answer.
- **No bullet that continues the one above it.** A bullet opening with
  "It", "This", or "The change" is the sentence-chopping failure.

## What happened

- **The index went live.** Brad pushed `af783b5`; the Pages deploy
  succeeded and yxeminutes.ca now serves it.
- **Card layout, `e080034`.** Five rows read as one block. The cause was
  spacing, not volume: gaps inside a row (0.2/0.25rem) were smaller than
  the gap between rows (0.5rem), and the two prose blocks sat at 0.85rem
  and 0.82rem — same size, same shape. Row padding to 0.9rem, description
  to 0.8rem, chip to 0.84rem plus a 2px left rule. **No text removed.**
  Brad's verdict: *"slightly better"* — which is why the bullets work
  above is the real fix.
- **Summaries no longer state the vote, `c475c8f`.** The chip eval had
  never passed — all three runs since it was written. Most flagged claims
  were the summary stating the verdict ("first reading passed 9-0") or
  contradicting it ("council has not yet decided" on an item that carried
  unanimously). An item carries several votes and the recorded tally
  belongs to only one.
- **The judge scores only what the model wrote, `6a6049f`.** It was
  judging the deterministic `Outcome` and `Vote Breakdown` chips, which
  `format_outcome` derives from the record and which have their own unit
  tests. The judge grounds a claim against a single field, so it read the
  **correct** "First reading passed (9-0)" as unsupported — the tally is
  in `vote_result`, the first-reading motion is in the agenda notes, and
  it will not combine them. Three items scored ≤2 on chips that match the
  record exactly. Scoped to `SEMANTIC_CATEGORIES`, the mean went from
  3.88–4.42 to **4.46–4.58**.
