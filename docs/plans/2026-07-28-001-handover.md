## The detail page — Brad's open question, with numbers

He asked whether the vertical space is spent on things that deserve it.
Measured on the June 24 council meeting:

| | count |
|---|---|
| rendered cards | **73** |
| of which substantive | **16** |
| procedural | 19 |
| section headers | 20 |
| consent | 18 |
| rows with **no** written description | **44 of 73** |
| rows with 4+ attachments | 13 |
| longest raw agenda content on one row | 2,089 chars |

So **53% of the page is procedural rows and section headers drawn at the
same visual weight as a decision** — "NATIONAL ANTHEM AND CALL TO ORDER"
gets a full card with a DESCRIPTION box, as does a $95M leisure centre.

And the page **contradicts itself**: the header says "43 agenda items"
(`count_agenda_items`, which excludes headers and procedural rows) and
then draws 73 cards.

Not designed yet. Ask Brad before building — he ratifies with a concrete
before/after, and `AskUserQuestion` with mock previews is what has
unstuck this three times now. Obvious candidates, in rough order:

- Collapse or de-weight procedural rows and section headers.
- Make the header's count and the rendered rows agree.
- Bound the attachment list before "Show more".

## Also open on the detail page

**A speaker has no play button and no anchor.** The play button is
item-level (`item.time_start_ms`); the speakers block is one section of
cards. A reader can jump to the item but never to the moment a
particular person started talking — which is the thing worth hearing.

Doing it needs a per-speaker timestamp, which we do not extract. The
cheapest route: have the speaker pass return a short verbatim phrase
from each speaker's opening, then locate that phrase in the transcript
segments to derive a millisecond offset. Untried.

## Traps

- **The speaker pass must see the whole transcript slice.** It was
  capped at 12,000 characters and reported **1 of 5** speakers on the
  Downtown Event District item: the first was at offset 8,017 and the
  rest began at 15,360. The model was not missing them, it was never
  shown them. Do not reintroduce a cap.
- **Speech-to-text destroys names.** "Aebig" appears zero times in the
  transcript of an item he spoke to for several minutes; "Chamber"
  appears nine. Match speakers by organization and self-introduction,
  never by spelling. This is written into the prompt — keep it there.
- **A registered speaker who did not speak must come back empty.**
  Timothy Cain registered on June 24 and has no remarks in the
  transcript; the correct output is nothing, not a plausible paraphrase.
- **`git commit` fails on an unchanged re-save** — fixed at `53b279b`,
