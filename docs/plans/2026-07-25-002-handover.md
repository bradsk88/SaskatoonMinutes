## What happened

### U8 — prompt rewrite, validated

Applied the long-pending prompt critique. **It took two iterations and
the first was a regression** (faithfulness 4.00 on a 4.0 gate). Final:

| | pre-U8 | post-U8 |
|---|---|---|
| descriptions over 220 chars | 7/11 | **0/11** |
| faithfulness | 4.82 | 4.82 |
| chips | 55 | 49–52 |
| items with a soft chip | 11/11 | 9–10/11 |

Faithfulness **recovered, it did not improve**. Real wins: overruns,
the `Colbison` name bug, truncated chips, and descriptions that open
"Saskatoon will build a storm sewer…" instead of "The item approves…".
Cost: ~10% fewer chips, at least two of them good.

### The cleanup A/B — cleanup is losing

`scripts/ab_cleanup.py` (new) extracts every eligible fixture item twice
— cached CleanTranscript vs raw slice — and writes blind pairs.
`scripts/ab_judge.py` (new) scores both arms with the Gemini judge.

- Gemini judge: 4.56 vs 4.11, but **the entire delta is one item**.
  Excluding it: 4.50 vs 4.50, dead even.
- Three blind non-Gemini judges: **raw 4, clean 2, tie 3** by per-item
  majority. All three picked raw on the *same four items*.

**The mechanism, confirmed:** the cleaned arm invented "Remai Modern" as
a letter-writer. The raw transcript says "Remly" (a condo corporation,
almost certainly Rumely); "Remai Modern" appears nowhere in it — and
*is* in `_SASKATOON_NAMES`. `_build_cleanup_prompt` says "CORRECT
garbled proper nouns to the closest match from this list", so an
out-of-roster name was snapped onto the nearest roster member. This is
the pass working as designed, not a random hallucination.

Consequence recorded in the plan: **roster expansion is cancelled.**
Every added name is another attractor. If cleanup survives U9, the fix
is conservative correction (only rewrite when the target also appears in
that item's official text), not a bigger list.

## Traps

- **Never judge an A/B by the A/B labels.** `blind_orders` randomizes
  per item, so "A" is the clean arm on some items and the raw arm on
  others. All three blind judges wrote cross-item conclusions about "A"
  and "B" — every one of those paragraphs is worthless. Aggregate only
  after unblinding. Per-item verdicts are fine.
- **Isolate before you attribute.** The committed baseline predated
  `2752c7f`, so the first U8 diff showed Outcome flips that were that
  older fix, not the prompt. `git stash push app/item_categorizer.py`,
  `--snapshot`, restore, `--diff` gives a true prompt-vs-prompt read.
- **Word budgets need calibrating, not converting.** 220 chars → "35
  words" was *looser* than the original (civic prose ≈ 7 chars/word) and
  changed nothing. 30 works.
- **Unpaired scores lie.** One arm of one item failed its judge call and
  the survivor was averaged in anyway — enough to reverse the sign of
  the headline at n=9. `pair_up` now drops half-scored items.
- **The judge source must carry `motion_text`/`vote_result`/
  `vote_detail`.** Omitting them made every "the body approved X"
  unsupported by construction; faithfulness collapsed to 1 on both arms.
- **`ab_cleanup.py` refuses to re-clean** on a cache miss, deliberately —
  re-cleaning spends the exact tokens the script exists to question.
  Run `eval_chips.py` first to populate CleanTranscripts.
- `--check` thresholds (`MIN_SOFT_COVERAGE`, `MAX_TITLE_ECHO`) are tuned
  for n=11. Widening the fixtures may trip them; that is a threshold
  problem, not a regression.
