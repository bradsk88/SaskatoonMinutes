## What happened

The user reported a council/Transportation recording was "ready but the
transcription job won't run for 8 hours." The investigation found the
real gap, then fixed it.

- **The gap.** The transcribe job iterated only `list_past`, which
  returns **passed** meetings. A meeting whose recording is up but
  eSCRIBE marks not-passed (HasVideo `True`, MeetingPassed `False`) was
  invisible to it, so it waited for the upstream flag, not the next run.
  The 8 hours was the daily cron, but the cron was never the bottleneck
  for a not-passed recording.
- **Fix (committed `6f2ba55`).** Added `MeetingSource.list_recorded(start,
  end)` — reads `GetCalendarMeetings`, returns meetings that have a video
  and are not passed (the exact gap). `EscribeMeetingSource` implements it
  (drops passed, videoless, and out-of-window meetings); `InMemoryMeetingSource`
  gained a `recorded` list. `transcribe_meetings.py` runs a calendar pass
  after the per-tab loop, deduped by meeting id, capped at `--limit`,
  default window 45 days back to 15 days forward. New fixture + test.
- **Cron (committed `a02d7c8`).** `30 */4 * * *` (every 4 hours), kept the
  minute-30 offset so it is staggered from the deploy's `0 */4` runs.
- **The calendar pass worked live.** The user's manual transcribe trigger
  (2026-09-01 23:33:48) ran the calendar pass and transcribed the
  Transportation meeting (`c046eacd-c3c7-4351-b210-6411713e005e`) —
  committed to the `transcripts` branch at **2026-09-01 23:38:30**.
- **The cascade ran.** Summarize 23:38:36, Deploy 23:40:26, both
  `success`. The Transportation meeting is also summarized on the
  `summaries` branch (commits 2026-08-28, 2026-08-29, and 2026-09-01
  10:17:39 — the summarize job covers scheduled/not-passed meetings via
  `list_scheduled`).
- **But the site still shows it "has not happened yet," and the tab is not
  lit.** Verified cause: eSCRIBE still marks the meeting
  `MeetingPassed: False` (2026-09-01: HasVideo `True`, HasAgenda `True`,
  MeetingPassed `False`). The display state is driven by eSCRIBE's
  MeetingPassed flag, **independent of the pipeline**. The meeting is
  fully transcribed and summarized, but it stays on the Future tab
  ("has not happened yet") until eSCRIBE marks it passed.

## Traps

- **The "has not happened yet" display is eSCRIBE's MeetingPassed flag,
  not the pipeline.** A meeting can be fully transcribed and summarized
  yet still read "has not happened yet." Do not re-diagnose the
  Transportation observation as a pipeline failure — the pipeline
  (transcribe → summarize → deploy) ran end-to-end at 23:38–23:40.
- **The two jobs have different inputs.** Transcribe reads `list_past`
  (passed) + the calendar (`list_recorded`, not-passed-but-recorded).
  Summarize reads `list_past` + `list_scheduled` (scheduled/not-passed).
  Do not assume "transcribe saw it ⇒ summarize saw it."
- **The 8 hours was the daily cron, not the real gap.** The real gap was
  the meeting not being in the job's input set (not-passed). The calendar
  pass fixed that; the cron is now 4 hours, but the cron was never the
  bottleneck for a not-passed recording.
- **eSCRIBE `HasVideo: True` and `MeetingPassed: False` are independent
  flags.** HasVideo means the recording is up; MeetingPassed controls the
  future-vs-past display. A meeting can have both at once.
- **The repo is public → Actions minutes are unlimited → runner time is
  free.** Do not assert a model-download or runner-time "cost."
