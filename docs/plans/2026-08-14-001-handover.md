## What happened

Problem (from prior handoff `/tmp/handoff-speaker-timestamps.md`, may
be gone): G&P 2026-08-12 heard items 6.3.2 and 7.1 as one discussion;
every speaker got the same timestamp on both cards — one speech
answered both, but it read as a leak.

Shipped in `651c4f0`:

- `app/speakers.py` `mark_jointly_heard` — detects jointly-heard items
  at build time. Four conditions, all required: window overlap ≥ 50% of
  the shorter window; section numbers not nested ("6." vs "6.1");
  both items have rosters; rosters share ≥1 name. Annotates each
  grouped item with `heard_with` (primary_item_id, primary_section,
  partners); rosters in the data are untouched.
- `app/templates/meeting.html` — primary card (earliest window start)
  renders the merged roster, one row per person in speaking order,
  titled "heard together with item 7.1"; partner cards render a
  pointer: "Heard together with item 6.3.2 — speakers are listed there."
- `scripts/build_site.py` — calls `mark_jointly_heard(items)` after the
  per-item speaker passes, unconditionally (detection needs bookmarks +
  rosters, not the transcript).
- ADR `docs/adr/0025-jointly-heard-items-share-one-speaker-list.md`;
  term "Jointly-Heard Items" in CONTEXT.md.

Evidence:

- Overlap alone groups 228/255 meetings (sloppy bookmarks); the
  shared-name condition reduced that to exactly the true pair on the
  live pages tested (G&P 2026-08-12 ✓, councils 2026-07-29 and
  2026-06-24 ✓ no groups).
- New tests: `tests/test_jointly_heard.py` (6 tests, incl. the G&P
  ground-truth windows 7:08–3:36:29 vs 1:10:51–3:08:50).
- Node smoke test of the merge/pointer JS passed (dedupe by name,
  stamp ordering, no-show collapse).
