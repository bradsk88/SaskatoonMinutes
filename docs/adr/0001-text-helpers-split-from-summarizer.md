# Text helpers are split from `summarizer.py` / `item_categorizer.py`

`app/agenda_text.py` (string shape: entity decoding, money formatting, title plainification, chip trimming), `app/transcript_text.py` (filler stripping, sentence splitting), and `app/agenda_items.py` (domain interpretation: procedural detection, outcome formatting, topic categorization) were extracted from `summarizer.py` and `item_categorizer.py` on 2026-05-01 (commit `ffc51ac`). Before the split, `summarizer.py` was ~300 lines mixing topic-ranking orchestration with string-shape concerns, and the same helpers were duplicated or cross-imported between the two callers. The split is deliberate and follows CONTEXT.md's distinction between text shape and domain interpretation — future architecture reviews should not propose re-merging on the basis of "module-hopping" or "shallow helpers" alone. If `summarizer.py`'s orchestration feels under-tested, the fix is to test the orchestration directly, not to inline the helpers.
